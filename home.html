THROUGH THE AGES

Explore Intel's journey through time, discovering how our commitment to innovation has shaped a more sustainable future for technology and our planet.

1968

Intel Founded

Intel was founded by Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore, beginning a new era in semiconductor technology.

1971

First Microprocessor

Intel introduced the 4004, the world's first commercial microprocessor.

1978

8086 Processor

Intel launched the 8086 processor, creating the foundation for modern personal computers.

1985

386 Processor

Intel introduced the 386 processor with 32-bit architecture, improving performance and multitasking.

1993

Pentium Processor

Intel introduced the Pentium processor, delivering faster performance and helping drive the growth of personal computing.

2006

Energy Efficiency Focus

Intel increased its focus on energy-efficient processors, reducing power consumption while improving performance.

2012

Water Conservation

Intel expanded water conservation programs and recycling initiatives across manufacturing facilities worldwide.

2020

100% Renewable Electricity

Intel achieved its goal of using 100% renewable electricity across its U.S. operations.
